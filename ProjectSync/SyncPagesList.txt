For Challenge 2 — **AI Service Orchestrator for Informal Economy** — your app should feel like a complete AI-powered service ecosystem, not just a booking app.
The UI flow should clearly demonstrate:

* user request
* AI understanding
* provider matching
* AI reasoning
* booking simulation
* follow-up automation
* live workflow tracking

Based on the hackathon requirements  and your feature breakdown , here’s the complete page structure with detailed functionality.
