# HelperHive: AI Service Orchestrator

## What the Project is About
**HelperHive** is a next-generation, AI-powered home services marketplace designed for the informal economy. Unlike standard booking apps, it functions as a complete **AI Service Orchestrator**. It connects users with local service providers (electricians, plumbers, AC technicians, beauticians, tutors, etc.) using an advanced multi-agent AI system. 

The AI handles the heavy lifting: interpreting natural language requests (including Roman Urdu), extracting intents, finding and ranking the best providers, predicting required materials, and even negotiating availability. The result is a seamless ecosystem with live tracking, automated follow-ups, and an intuitive interface that empowers both customers and local informal economy workers.

---

## Summary of Listed Features & Pages

The project documentation outlines a comprehensive system divided into three main pillars: **Design System**, **User Application**, and **Provider Application**.

### 1. Design & UI/UX System
- **Aesthetic:** Modern, mobile-first SaaS design featuring a "Soft UI" with rounded corners (up to 32px), subtle depth, and high-quality 3D/isometric illustrations.
- **Branding:** Vibrant, trust-inspiring Green-centric palette (Primary: `#34A853`).
- **Typography & Layout:** Built on an 8px grid system, utilizing clean fonts (Inter/Plus Jakarta Sans) with accessible, high-contrast dark and light modes.
- **Components:** Custom map pins, range sliders, chat bubbles, and 5-star amber rating displays.

### 2. User-Side Flow (Customer Experience)
The user app features over 20 distinct screens designed to map out the entire customer journey:
- **Onboarding & Auth:** Splash screens, guest flows, and secure login/signup, along with precise map-based address setup.
- **AI-Powered Discovery:** A home dashboard featuring a chat-like natural language input and voice recording for service requests.
- **AI Orchestration Workflow:** A simulated live page where users watch AI agents (Intent, Location, Provider Search, Ranking) process their request in real time.
- **Booking & Comparison:** Side-by-side provider comparisons, AI reasoning breakdowns (why a provider was matched), and seamless booking confirmation.
- **Live Tracking & Chat:** Real-time map tracking of the technician, ETA updates, and an in-app chat equipped with image sharing, voice notes, and AI-generated suggested replies/summaries.
- **Post-Service Automation:** AI-generated reminders, rebooking suggestions, dual feedback/rating systems, and digital cash-receipt logs.

### 3. Provider-Side Flow (Worker/Shop Experience)
A dedicated interface for service providers to manage their business and workers:
- **Shop & Worker Management:** Provider registration, service radius setup, and tools to manage individual workers with specific specialization tags.
- **Smart Dashboard:** An overview of active bookings, pending requests, daily revenue, and worker availability.
- **AI Request Processing:** When a request comes in, the AI provides the shop with a summary of the customer's issue and predicts the tools/materials needed before arrival.
- **Automated Negotiation:** An AI scheduling assistant that automatically negotiates alternative time slots with customers if the provider's requested time is booked.

---

**In short:** HelperHive bridges the gap between traditional informal labor and modern technology by using AI to intelligently route, match, and manage local services from request to completion.
